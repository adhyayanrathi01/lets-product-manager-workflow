# Meeting-analysis rules

- Allow standalone analysis by date range, meeting type, project, module, feature, participant group, or topic.
- Discover available notes, transcripts, recordings, calendars, or documents at runtime; do not assume a provider.
- Separate confirmed decisions from proposals, discussion, and agent inference.
- Preserve attribution where useful while minimizing unnecessary personal data.
- Do not send summaries, schedule meetings, or create tasks without explicit approval.
