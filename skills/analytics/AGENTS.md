# Analytics rules

- Discover the available analytics access at runtime; do not assume a provider.
- Confirm the metric definition, time range, user segment, and unit of analysis.
- Identify and exclude test, employee, bot, demo, and internal activity.
- Inspect event semantics and identity behavior before drawing conclusions.
- Check observation-window maturity, right-censoring, sample sufficiency, and decision-specific practical significance.
- Separate observed behavior from causal explanations.
- Save reusable quantitative findings through the shared evidence handoff.
- Record reusable analytics conventions only after they are confirmed.
