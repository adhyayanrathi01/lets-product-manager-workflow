# Support-ticket rules

- Allow standalone analysis by date range, queue, module, feature, segment, tag, status, or issue type.
- Discover the available ticket source at runtime; do not assume a provider.
- Exclude spam, automated notifications, merged duplicates, and irrelevant operational tickets where possible.
- Preserve source coverage and distinguish ticket volume from unique affected customers or accounts.
- Do not change ticket status, ownership, priority, or replies without explicit approval.
