# Team-communication rules

- Allow standalone analysis by date range, channel, thread, team, module, feature, topic, or decision.
- Discover available communication sources at runtime; do not assume a provider.
- Respect source permissions, private-channel boundaries, and the requested participant scope.
- Exclude bots, automated alerts, and irrelevant conversation where possible.
- Call a decision confirmed only when the source records explicit approval from an authorized owner; otherwise label it proposed or unclear.
- Do not send messages, replies, reactions, or email without explicit approval.
- Use the company communication style from context.md when available.
